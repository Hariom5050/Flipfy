Supported now

ZIP flipbooks are scanned for page images or PDFs. Existing PDF flipbooks are copied directly. Image sequences are sorted by filename and assembled into a high-quality PDF.

Handled safely

SWF and proprietary flipbook packages are detected and rejected with a clear message because modern browsers cannot parse them safely without extra runtimes.

Session only

Flipfy does not use localStorage, cookies, analytics, forms, or network requests. Closing the tab clears the working state.

Why I Built Flipfy

I built Flipfy because converting a flipbook into a PDF should be easy, but many tools require uploads, limit free conversions, hide basic features behind subscriptions, show too many ads, or ask for accounts for no good reason.

A fast, free, privacy-first Flipbook to PDF converter that works entirely on your own device.

Your files never leave your computer. No uploads. No servers. No tracking. No accounts.
