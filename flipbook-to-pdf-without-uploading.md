# How to Convert a Flipbook to PDF Without Uploading Files

Converting a flipbook to PDF should not require sending your files to an unknown server. Many online converters ask users to upload documents, create accounts, wait through free limits, or pay before downloading. If your flipbook contains study material, school files, notes, or private documents, uploading it may not be the best option.

Flipfy offers a private alternative: convert flipbooks to PDF directly inside your browser.

## What Is Flipfy?

Flipfy is a free browser-based flipbook to PDF converter. It helps convert supported flipbook files into standard PDF files without server uploads.

You open the website, choose your flipbook file, convert it, and download the PDF.

Try Flipfy: https://flipfy.netlify.app/

## Supported Formats

Flipfy supports:

- ZIP flipbooks containing page images or PDFs
- Existing PDF flipbooks
- Image page sequences such as JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF

## How To Convert A Flipbook To PDF

1. Open Flipfy at https://flipfy.netlify.app/
2. Drop your flipbook file into the upload area, or choose it from your device.
3. Click **Convert to PDF**.
4. Wait for the browser to process the file.
5. Download the generated PDF.
